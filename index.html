Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What services are available on AkPrintHub?

At AkPrintHub (also known as AK Print Hub, Ak Print Hub or AkPrint Hub) you can use a variety of online services like converting JPG to PDF, formatting documents like Aadhaar Card and Voter ID Card for print, creating passport size photos, and designing wedding cards or banners.

Are all these tools free to use?

Many of our basic tools on AkPrintHub / AK Print Hub are absolutely free. Some advanced services, such as all types of ID card printing, are available under our Pro Plan, which comes at very affordable prices.

What should be the size of the photo to make a passport photo?

You don't have to worry about the size. Simply upload any good quality photo to Ak Print Hub (AkPrintHub), and our tool will automatically convert it to standard passport size (usually 3.5cm x 4.5cm) and prepare it to print on an A4 sheet.

Are my uploaded files safe?

Yes, of course. Your safety is our first priority at AkPrintHub. All your files are permanently deleted from our servers within a few hours of being processed. We do not store or share any of your files.
